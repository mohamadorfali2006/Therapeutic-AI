# AI Research & Machine Learning — 14 roles

**Reports to:** CTO. **Leads:** VP AI Research.

| Role | Count | Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| VP, AI Research | 1 | Model strategy, roadmap, research quality |
| Research Scientist — Foundation Models | 3 | Protein folding/co-folding, large pretrained models |
| Research Scientist — Molecular Generation | 3 | Generative chemistry, ligand/design-optimization models |
| Research Scientist — Predictive Modeling | 3 | Docking, ADMET, property prediction, systems biology |
| ML Scientist — Evaluation/Alignment | 2 | Proper scoring, calibration, OOD detection, bias |

## Key principle
**Evaluation scientists are separate from modeling scientists** so validation isn't self-serving — critical for scientific credibility and for FDA/PCCP evidence.

## Candidate profile
- PhD (or equivalent) in ML, computational biology/chemistry, biophysics.
- Foundational-model experience (protein/generative chemistry), PyTorch fluency.
- Publications desirable; regulated-AI exposure a plus.
