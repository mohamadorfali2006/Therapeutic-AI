## Purpose
The domain experts who hold AI models to a **biological standard** — pairing with AI Research to keep predictions grounded in real drug-discovery practice.

## Candidate profile
- PhD in computational chemistry, cheminformatics, structural biology (or equivalent industry experience).
- 5–8+ years in pharma/biotech/CRO; modern ML methods in drug discovery (generative design, docking, ADMET).
- Program leadership from hit finding to lead optimization a plus for the Head role.
