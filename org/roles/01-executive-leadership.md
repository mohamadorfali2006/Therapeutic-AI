# Executive Leadership — 6 roles

| Role | Reports to | Mandate |
|---|---|---|
| CEO | Board | Vision, fundraising, business development, ultimate accountability |
| Chief Scientific Officer (CSO) | CEO | Scientific strategy, internal pipeline, external scientific credibility |
| Chief Technology Officer (CTO) | CEO | All software/ML, product architecture, SaMD engineering |
| Chief Medical Officer (CMO) | CEO | Clinical validation, medical affairs, real-world evidence |
| Chief Regulatory & Quality Officer (CRQO) | CEO | FDA 510(k)/De Novo, PCCP, EU MDR/AI Act, QMS |
| Chief Operating Officer (COO) | CEO | People, finance, legal, business process, scale |

## Notes
- **Most important early hire sequence:** CEO, then **CSO + CRQO together** — do not start the SaMD clock late.
- CRQO owns QMS (ISO 13485) and the Regulated-AI function (matrixed with CTO).
- CMO owns clinical affairs; RA/QA report through CRQO.
