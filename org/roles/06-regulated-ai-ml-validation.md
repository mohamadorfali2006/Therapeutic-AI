## Why this team is non-negotiable
FDA's **PCCP** (final guidance Dec 2024) lets you retrain iteratively **only if** you can prove each change follows a pre-authorized Modification Protocol. Without a CI/CD-style validation pipeline, PCCP is a paper plan you can't execute. This team converts "adaptive AI" from hope into approved reality.

## Independence
Reports to Regulatory/Quality (not pure engineering) so **validation stays independent of shipping pressure**.

- Software/ML validation; IEC 62304, ISO 13485 familiarity.
- Data lineage/governance engineering; regulatory submission experience a strong plus.
