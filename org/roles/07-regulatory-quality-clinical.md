## Regulatory reality (2026)
- **~90%** of FDA AI device authorizations are via **510(k)**; De Novo for first-in-class.
- **PCCP** (final guidance Dec 2024, implementation guidance Aug 2025) enables adaptive retraining with pre-authorization.
- FDA cybersecurity guidance is now final (2023); SBOM/pen-test expected.
- **EU:** MDR + EU AI Act (high-risk obligations ~Aug 2026, proposed delay to Dec 2027). Run EU parallel to FDA.

## Strategy for this company
- Drug-discovery tools (research) → **non-SaMD** (lighter regulatory).
- Clinical insights serving at point of care → **SaMD** via 510(k) or De Novo.
- **PCCP-first** submission design; QA owns ISO 13485 QMS + IEC 62304 alignment.
