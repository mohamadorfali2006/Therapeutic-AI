# Business / GTM / Operations — Charter

## Mission

Make the Drug Discovery Engine (DDE) a viable research-stage product: define how the company proposes to make money, position the DDE in the market, secure early research customers, and keep the company financially runnable through the current research phase. We do not sell therapy yet; we sell credible access to a research platform and build the pipeline that later converts into pharma co-development and SaMD revenue.

We report to the COO per `org/roles/09-business-gtm-operations.md` and operate under `company/00-OPERATING-MODEL.md`.

## Scope

| Area | Owned artifacts | What we own |
|---|---|---|

| Pricing concepts | `pricing-mock-*.md` (mock widgets, never live prices) | Staged pricing model thinking |

**Not owned:** ML internals (`ai-research/`), regulatory claims (`regulatory/`, `regulated-ai/`), product code. We feed input and consume outcomes.

## Go-to-market concept

### (a) Positioning

> "Drug Discovery Engine — an AI drug discovery research platform. Research use only. Not for clinical decision-making."

- The DDE accelerates target-to-candidate exploration with predictive models, provenance, and a validation spine built in.
- Every external claim repeats the research-use-only disclaimer (see `03-guardrails.md`).
- We sell the platform's credibility: transparent provenance, reproducible evaluations — not hype about new drugs.

### (b) Target early customers

| Segment | Who | Entry offer |
|---|---|---|
| Biotech R&D teams | Early-stage biotechs with internal chemistry pipelines | Research tier: batch prediction + model monitoring dashboard |
| Academic labs | University labs in comp-chem / DMPK | Pilot/research tier, co-authored case studies |
| CROs | Contract research orgs running paid screening | White-label evaluation runs on their data (future) |

Priority now: a small cohort of design partners (1-3) rather than broad acquisition.

### (c) Staged revenue model

| Stage | Product | Revenue mechanism | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Now (research) | DDE SaaS research tier | Platform subscription for research teams | Proposed, unrealistic revenue this phase |
| Next | DDE + pharma co-development | Upstream milestones from partnered drug programs | Pipeline only |
| Later | SaMD clinical tools | FDA-cleared software sales (PCCP-first) | Not started — regulatory track must approve |

Revenue claims require evidence (see `03-guardrails.md`): we propose the model, we do not book revenue.

### (d) Single-product focus

The DDE is the **single primary product** per the operating model. All GTM, pricing, and ops work orbits one thing. Any new business idea must attach to the DDE or be explicitly deferred — no side bets.
